Q2 — How They Did It
First, he showed that if he multiplied all the speeds together, the product would be divisible by several specific prime numbers. Second, he went to an amended version of Tao's threshold, which said that, in short, if the conjecture was true for products up a a given limit, it was true for all. Strength: Excellent. She actually walks through both steps: (1) prime divisibility constraint, (2) Tao's threshold. At age 12, this level of logical reconstruction from a math article is impressive.
Growth area: Minor — "an amended version" should be "a modified version." She could add: why this approach (proof by contradiction) is more powerful than trying to prove it directly.

Growth area: The analysis stays on the surface of what the quotes say rather than what they reveal. She could push into: why does being laughed at matter so much to Orwell? What does that fear tell us about the psychology of power?

That is because this story is about how Orwell was controlled by the power of the crowd, against his better judgement.
